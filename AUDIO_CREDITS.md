# Audio credits

All ambient tracks are real recordings, not synthesized audio. The app stores
the prepared clips as stereo 44.1 kHz WAV files for Android playback.

- **Daytime birds:** [Birds Chirping 75156](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/birds-chirping-75156/),
  available under the Pixabay Content License. The selected recording is used
  as a 22.4-second ambient bed and looped by the player.
- **Rain:** [Rain on roofs](https://freesound.org/people/xkeril/sounds/669487/)
  by xkeril, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  The first 50 seconds are used, with a short fade at the loop boundary.
- **Night:** [FrogsAndCrickets Excerpt B](https://freesound.org/people/greysound/sounds/32655/)
  by greysound, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  A 30-second section containing the pond frogs and crickets is used and
  looped with short fades.
